About this analysis 3 scores per server · compared to same-shift peers · ≥12 shifts · coaching diagnoses, Actions verifies ▾
How it works
Each server is scored on three things, independent of each other: Revenue (do they upsell premium items, modifiers, larger orders?), Retention (do guests come back to them?), and Reliability (low comp / void / complaint rate). Servers are only compared to peers working the same daypart, section, and location. Minimum 12 shifts in the last 60 days, and at least 3 peer servers, before we'll rank anyone.

Sample
14 servers analyzed · 8 shown above (clear pattern) · 6 hidden (average performers — coaching effort wouldn't produce meaningful lift).

Doctrine
Server Coaching diagnoses. Turn coaching opportunities into actions — the lifecycle lives in Actions. Recognition and shadow-training are operator-led. Verified savings only after coaching changes are implemented and revenue lift is measured against guardrails.

Detection

How labor leaks are surfaced — formulas, sources, confidence, blockers
ƒThe formulas ▾
Sales per labor hour (RPLH) = Net sales ÷ labor hours Extra hours = scheduled hours − hours needed Hours needed = guests ÷ guests-per-server target Weekly $ = extra hrs × loaded wage × services/wk Loaded wage = base + tax + workers' comp + benefits Monthly run-rate = weekly × 4.33

Sales per labor hour tells you where the gap is. The dollar amount comes from extra hours × the loaded wage — not from the productivity number itself.

Verification

Guardrails enforced before estimated savings become verified
◎Guardrails (verification-side) ▾
Ticket time must not worsen during monitoring window. Measured per service vs baseline.
Guest sentiment must not drop. Tracked via review platforms + complaint patterns.
Sales / throughput must not fall. If covers drop >5% with staffing held constant, guardrail trips.
Manager override allowed at any point. Pauses monitoring window; resumes on resume.

Staffing reduction is not counted as verified if service quality degrades, even if labor cost dropped.

Server Coaching

Three-axis methodology + the use restrictions baked into the product
⊕Methodology ▾

Primary metric: Premium item attach rate.

Attach % = checks w/ premium tag ÷ total checks

Secondary metrics: items/check, modifier capture, comp rate.

Peer group:

Same daypart × section type × location. Min 3 peer servers required.

Sample floor:

At least 12 shifts in the last 60 days per server. Below that, no ranking.

Who we show you:

The top quarter (your standout performers) and the bottom quarter (your coaching opportunities). The middle 50% is intentionally hidden — coaching effort on average performers doesn't produce meaningful lift, and surfacing them invites misuse.

Deliberately excluded:

Tip percentage. Correlates with section assignment and party demographics, not service quality. Including it would generate structurally biased rankings.
Total tips, total sales. Confounded by section, daypart, party size. Not server-attributable.
Composite "performance score". Hides what's being measured. Each metric stays decomposable.
⊗Use restrictions ▾

Per SKC Terms of Service, Server Coaching outputs may not be used as the sole input for:

— Scheduling decisions (which servers get which shifts)
— Promotion or demotion decisions
— Disciplinary action
— Termination

Allowed use cases:

— Coaching conversations and training
— Shadow training pairing (top performer + new hire)
— Recognition and morale
— Identifying section/daypart structural issues

Final personnel decisions remain with the operator. SKC data is one input among many.

Trust & Safety

What the LLM is — and isn't — permitted to do with labor data
⚠LLM restrictions ▾

Claude (Sonnet) generates labor analysis and natural-language summaries, but is not permitted to:

— Set or modify confidence scores. Confidence is rule-driven.
— Override RPLH thresholds or wage assumptions.
— Call estimated values "saved" or "recovered" in any output.
— Auto-create actions. Operator approval required.
— Generate language about a server's character, attitude, or perceived motivation. Only measurable behavior in metrics.
— Compare servers across different peer groups. Daypart/section/location must match.

LLM outputs are decision-support only. Final authority is the operator.
