# Reports — screen spec

> The owner-ready weekly summary artifact: verified wins, active recovery, open
> exposure, per-location breakdown, and what changed — the "GM-to-owner" weekly
> report, one click instead of hand-assembly.

- **File:** `screens/reports.html`
- **DOM id:** `screen-reports` · **route:** `showScreen('reports', …)`
- **Group:** Prove
- **Status:** seeded

## Purpose
The weekly owner report. It assembles, in one click, the cadence summary an owner
expects — verified wins (counted), active recovery (in progress), open exposure,
a per-location breakdown, and what changed — so the GM doesn't have to build it by
hand. It exists to close the owner-reporting loop: owners want a regular artifact;
operators want it generated, not assembled.

## Access — who & when
- **Primary user:** Owner reading.
- **Secondary:** GM generating / scheduling.
- **When used:** weekly (typically Monday morning); monthly aggregate; quarterly for
  board meetings.

## Route & how it's reached
- Sidebar **Reports** item (Prove group): `showScreen('reports', this, 'Reports')`
  — _confirm exact label/handler against `components/sidebar.html`._
- Consumption can happen off-device via the exported PDF / emailed report.

## Subpages
None. Organized as in-page tabs via `switchTab('reports', …)` — keys `weekly`,
`verified`, `recovery`, `risks`, `timeline`.

## Page states
- **Loading:** _TBD — verify against `reports.html`._
- **Empty / cold-start:** no report generated yet / no verified lines — _confirm

## Data dependencies
- **Registries / fixtures:** pulls from **Verified** actions (counted), **Active
  Recovery** (in-progress, `getPortfolioTruth()` / `ACTIONS`), and **Profit
  Recovery** open exposure. Verified line binds via `data-skc="verified-savings*"`.
- **Output types shown:** `verified` (VER · green) on counted wins — the only type
  that counts; `active_recovery` (ACT · blue) and `open_opportunity` (OPEN · amber)
  shown separately and labeled as estimates.
- **Sources gating confidence:** the seven SKC sources behind each verified line and
  the open-exposure estimates — _confirm per-line gating against `reports.html`._

## Doctrine specifics
With ROI Proof, this is the second **verified-only** surface and the rule is
load-bearing. Per [docs/doctrine.md](../docs/doctrine.md), **only `verified` counts
toward ROI**; the screen states it verbatim ("Only verified wins count in ROI.
Active recovery and open exposure are estimates until each monitoring window closes
with all guardrails passing" and "Reports include only verified values. Estimates
are labeled and excluded from the ROI figure"). Active Recovery and Open Exposure
appear in their own tabs/cells so they **never inflate** the verified number.
Canonical numbers (see
[docs/canonical-numbers.md](../docs/canonical-numbers.md)):

| Value | Output type / state | Role on screen |
|---|---|---|
| **$97/wk** (≈ **$420/mo** run-rate equiv.) | `verified` · VER · green (shown VER · 88%) | "Verified this period," counted in ROI |
| **$820/mo** run-rate | `active_recovery` · ACT · blue | Active Recovery — Walnut Creek bar-staffing reduction, monitoring week 3/4, "not counted until verified" (closes May 24) |
| Open exposure | `open_opportunity` · OPEN · amber | Open-exposure cell/tab — estimated, not counted |

The presentation layer cannot upgrade an estimate to verified; type/confidence flow
data → render. The "Next to verify" note keeps the framing honest ("on pace to
verify next week" — not yet counted).

## Edge cases
- Active Recovery shown but excluded from ROI until its window closes clean
  (e.g. "If week 4 closes clean on May 24, this becomes $820/mo verified and counted
  in ROI").
- Risks tab surfaces OT/threshold items (e.g. "3 employees crossing OT threshold ·
  schedule cap resolves without staffing reduction").
